My Story

How it all started

My journey into tech began with curiosity — taking apart interfaces I admired and wondering how they worked. What started as late-night experiments with HTML and CSS grew into a genuine passion for building things people love to use.

Along the way I discovered that great software isn't just functional — it's thoughtful. Every animation, every spacing decision, every word of microcopy shapes how someone feels when they interact with your product.

Today I work at the intersection of design and engineering, partnering with startups and teams who want to raise the bar. Whether it's a design system, a marketing site, or a complex web app — I bring the same level of care to every project.
