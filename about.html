My Story

How it all started

My journey into tech started with curiosity and hardware — building computers at Jimm's PC-Store, updating BIOS firmware, and learning how systems work from the inside out. That hands-on foundation shaped how I approach software today.

Through game jams, hackathons, and theFIRMA project work at Turku AMK, I discovered that the best products come from collaborative teams. As Game Production Lead and Lead Designer, I learned production planning, scheduling, and how to ship under pressure.

Today I work as a Full Stack Engineer at Bittera Oy, building a remote digital signage platform on Raspberry Pi. I'm passionate about software engineering, full-stack development, and project management roles where I can grow as a developer and contribute to real-world products.
